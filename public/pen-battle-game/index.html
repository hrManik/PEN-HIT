Official Bench Rules

  • Two Players: Player 1 commands the classic Blue pen; Player 2 commands the sleek Red pen.
  • Turn-Based Aiming: Only the active player can shoot. Click or tap your pen, drag backward like a slingshot, and release.
  • Knockout (+1 Point & Bonus Turn): Knock the opponent's pen off the desk to earn 1 point. You also earn a consecutive bonus shot!
  • Miss or Incomplete: If you miss the opponent's pen or both pens remain on the desk, the turn passes to your opponent.
  • Foul Penalty (+1 Point to Opponent): If your own pen falls off the bench, you immediately lose your turn, your opponent is awarded +1 point, and both pens are reset to safe positions.
  • Double Fall Rule: If both pens fall off the desk on the same shot, it is ruled an over-thrust foul by the shooter: exactly 1 point is awarded to the opponent and the turn passes over. No double points are ever awarded.
  • Realistic Physics: Pens are capsule rigid bodies. Striking off-center induces spin torque and alters collision deflection angles!
